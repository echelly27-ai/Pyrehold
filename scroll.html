The first day

Three minutes before they come.

The opening sun lasts long enough to chop, quarry, and haul scrap. A dropper beside the hearth throws gold coins onto the dirt. Walk over a coin and it is yours. Press T only when the walls are ready.

  • Wood Pines in Ashpine. Walls, floors, and the first gun.
  • Stone Grey rock, and a quarry to the east.
  • Metal Scrap piles. Later forts and heavier guns.
  • Gold Coins from the dropper. Twelve a piece, as long as you pick them up.

The yard

Stand on the price. The camp builds itself.

Green pads ring the plot. Each one names a wall, floor, barricade, campfire, or watchtower and a pile of gold. Stay on the pad and the piece rises beside you. The amber stall is the weaponry: guns cost wood, stone, and gold.

When the light goes

They path for the hearth and chew whatever blocks them.

Wave one is a single lane of shamblers. Later nights add runners, spitters, and bosses. Die, and dawn snaps back: the yard empties, the same wave waits, and the camp you built is still standing.
